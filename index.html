            HOW TO PLAY
            
    
                
  • > The hidden word is built from the clue boxes, in order.
    • 
                
  • > You get 4 guesses. A guess can be any length.
    • 
                
  • > GREEN a part is in your guess, in the same spot (same distance from the left or right end).
    • 
                
  • > YELLOW a part is in your guess, but in the wrong spot.
    • 
                
  • > Guesses containing a part get sliced around it. No part, no slice.
    • 
                
  • > CLUE shows letter counts, then a hint, then the word.
    • 
                
  • > DAILY is one word a day and builds your streak. ARCHIVE replays past days. ZEN never ends.
