SLICE

Cut the word. Find the parts.

 

HOW TO PLAY
  • > The hidden word is built from the clue slots, in order.
  • > You get 4 guesses. A guess can be any length.
  • > GREEN your guess contains a part in the same spot (same distance from the left or right end).
  • > YELLOW your guess contains a part, but in the wrong spot.
  • > Stuck? CLUE shows letter counts, then a hint, then (if you're sure) the word.
  • > UNLIMITED gives you a fresh random word every round.
