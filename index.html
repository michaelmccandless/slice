HOW TO PLAY
  • > The hidden word is built from the clue chips, in order.
  • > You get 4 guesses. A guess can be any length.
  • > GREEN a part is in your guess, in the same spot (same distance from the left or right end).
  • > YELLOW a part is in your guess, but in the wrong spot.
  • > Guesses containing a part get sliced between the parts.
  • > Stuck? CLUE shows letter counts, then a hint, then the word.
  • > ZEN MODE: no daily limit. Relax and play forever.
